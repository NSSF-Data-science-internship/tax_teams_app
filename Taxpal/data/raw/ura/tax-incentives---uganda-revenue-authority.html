      WHT Exemption

      Withholding tax (WHT) is a form of income tax that is withheld at source by one person (withholding agent) upon making payment to another person (payee).

       

       

      Add to Bookmarks
      Please login to bookmark Close

      To apply, applicants are required to visit the URA web portal. Applicants are required to log into their TIN account using their TIN password. Under e-Services, click on e-Registration Tax Exemption Application, proceed with the application process to the end, and then submit.

       

      • Visit the URA portal and log in with your credentials.
      • Under e-services, select Taxpayer Profile.
      • Then click Print TIN Certificate.
      • The certificate will be downloaded as a PDF.
